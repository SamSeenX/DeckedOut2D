Decked Out 2D – Free Browser Dungeon Crawler Game

About Decked Out 2D

Decked Out 2D is a free-to-play browser-based dungeon crawler game inspired by the popular Hermitcraft season 9 minigame "Decked Out 2" created by Mr. Tango Tek. Navigate through dark, treacherous dungeons, avoid deadly monsters, collect valuable artifacts, and escape before your clank meter attracts too many enemies!

This 2D adaptation brings the thrilling stealth-action gameplay to your browser with no downloads required. Experience the tension of sneaking through dungeons, the excitement of finding rare artifacts, and the panic of being chased by relentless creatures.

How to Play – Game Controls

Master these controls to survive the dungeon:

  • WASD / Arrow Keys – Move your character through the dungeon
  • SHIFT (Hold) – Sneak mode for silent movement (reduces clank)
  • SPACEBAR – Jump over obstacles and gaps
  • F Key – Harvest berries from bushes or eat from inventory
  • E Key – Search for artifacts at marked locations

Game Features

  • 🏃 Stealth Gameplay – Your movements create "clank" that attracts enemies
  • 👹 Dangerous Enemies – Ravagers patrol corridors, Ghasts shoot fireballs, and Vexes spawn when clank is high
  • 🔥 Ember Collection – Gather embers scattered throughout the dungeon for points
  • 🍒 Survival Mechanics – Find and eat berries to restore health
  • 🧭 Compass Navigation – Follow your compass to locate artifacts and exits
  • 👑 Rare Artifacts – Discover unique treasures with different values
  • 📱 Mobile Friendly – Touch controls available for mobile devices

Tips for Beginners

  1. Sneak Often – Hold SHIFT when moving to reduce clank and avoid detection
  2. Watch Your Clank – Keep the clank meter low to prevent Vex spawns
  3. Collect Berries – Stock up on food before venturing deep into the dungeon
  4. Use the Compass – The red needle points toward your objective
  5. Time Your Jumps – Use SPACEBAR to hop over hazards and climb elevated terrain
  6. Find the Exit – After grabbing an artifact, follow your compass to escape!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Decked Out 2D free to play?

Yes! Decked Out 2D is completely free to play in your browser with no downloads or accounts required.

Does the game work on mobile?

Yes, enable "Touch Controls" on the start screen to play on smartphones and tablets.

What is "clank" in the game?

Clank is a noise meter that increases when you run, jump, or move quickly. High clank attracts dangerous Vex enemies, so sneak to stay safe!

How do I win the game?

Find the hidden artifact by pressing E at marked locations, then escape through the exit before the monsters get you!
