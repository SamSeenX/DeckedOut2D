The Legend of the Hollow

"You didn't mean to fall. No one ever does.

One moment you were exploring the ancient ruins mentioned in your grandfather's journal, and the next, the ground beneath you gave way. You tumbled into darkness, landing on cold, hard stone. When you stood up, the light from above was gone.

You are now in The Hollow, a subterranean labyrinth built by a civilization that vanished eons ago. The air is thick with the smell of frost and old magic. The walls shift, and creatures made of shadow and bone patrol the corridors.

A whisper in your mind reveals the only escape: The Gate of Exit. But the Gate demands a toll. It hungers for the 21 Cursed Artifacts hidden throughout the dungeon. Only by retrieving these relics can you buy your freedom.

But beware... the dungeon listens. If you move too fast, if you make too much noise... CLANK... the Wardens will wake."

Survival Guide

Surviving The Hollow requires more than speed; it requires cunning and silence.

🤫 The Clank System

Noise is your enemy. Every action generates "Clank."

  • Clank Meter: Located at the top left. Keep it low!
  • Generating Clank: Running and Jumping generate partial noise.
  • The Consequences: High Clank attracts more enemies and spawns Vexes (Phantoms) that fly through walls to hunt you.
  • Stealth: Hold SHIFT to Sneak. Sneaking generates 0 Clank and helps you evade detection.

👹 The Wardens

You are not alone in the dark.

  • Ravagers: Heavy beasts that patrol corridors. They have poor vision but excellent hearing. If they see you, run!
  • Ghasts: Floating skulls that guard open areas. They shoot fireballs if you enter their line of sight.
  • Vexes: Spirits summoned by high Clank. They can phase through walls. Lower your Clank to despawn them.

⚡ Vitality & Embers

  • Health: You have 3 Hearts. Taking damage knocks you back.
  • Berries (Food): Find bushes to harvest berries. Press F to eat and heal 1 Heart.
  • Embers: Glowing blue flames scattered around. Collect them to increase your score and prove your worth.
